# Brand Rules

⚠️ Read this before producing any training material, deck, marketing copy, or external-facing answer that references brand ownership.

## The rule

Apps marked **"10 Lives branded: No"** in `references/product-registry.md` must NEVER be described as "10 Lives Studios products" in training materials, decks, staff docs, sales conversations, or any external-facing content.

## What IS 10 Lives Studios branded

The following apps are explicitly 10 Lives Studios branded — Claude may use that branding for these only:

- **ARTAS** (`realitytelevisionawards.com`)
- **Tribeca-Film-Festival-2026** (event-specific 10 Lives property)

## What IS NOT 10 Lives Studios branded

Everything else in `references/product-registry.md`, including:

- Mythie (CastHub)
- Teleprompter
- Aclamos (Run of Show)
- The Production Shelf
- PH Invoicing
- Backlot Hub

These are owned by Andrew Ward / Toronado Entertainment, LLC, but are NOT 10 Lives Studios products. Do not attach the 10 Lives name to them.

## When in doubt

1. Check `references/product-registry.md` — the "10 Lives Branded?" column is canonical.
2. If an app is not in the registry, do not assume any brand — ask Andrew.
3. If a customer or staff member asks "is this a 10 Lives product?", answer based on the registry only.

## Why this matters

Mis-attributing brand creates:
- Legal/trademark exposure across distinct legal entities
- Customer confusion about who is responsible for the product
- Marketing inconsistency that erodes brand value across both Toronado Entertainment and 10 Lives Studios properties

Treat this rule as absolute, not proportional to audience size.
