# Minimum Required Clauses — Per Document Type

Use as a checklist when auditing or drafting. Missing any required clause is a RED finding on Lane A.

---

## Privacy Policy (UK GDPR / EU GDPR Art. 13 baseline)

- [ ] Identity and contact details of data controller (legal name, registered address, contact email) — **Toronado Entertainment, LLC** for all apps except Producing Hollywood
- [ ] If EU users accepted: EU representative named (or explicit no-EU-users statement)
- [ ] What personal data is collected
- [ ] Why it is collected (purpose) and legal basis for processing (Art. 6)
- [ ] If special-category data: Art. 9 legal basis explicit
- [ ] How long data is retained (or criteria for determining retention)
- [ ] Whether data is shared with third parties (named vendors — must match `legal-inventory.json#vendors`)
- [ ] Cross-border transfer mechanism (adequacy decision, SCCs, UK IDTA) — US transfers must name a valid mechanism
- [ ] User rights: access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, restriction, withdraw consent
- [ ] Right to lodge a complaint with the ICO (`ico.org.uk`) — UK; lead supervisory authority if EU
- [ ] CCPA/CPRA "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link if US users + any sharing
- [ ] US state-law disclosures for VA / CO / CT / UT / TX / FL / OR / MT etc., or one combined "US State Residents" section
- [ ] Cookie/tracking disclosure or link to Cookie Policy
- [ ] Contact email for data subject requests (must be live)
- [ ] Effective date / last updated date
- [ ] Version identifier matching `legal-inventory.json#currentPrivacyVersion`

---

## Terms of Service (US + UK consumer-facing baseline)

- [ ] Description of the service
- [ ] Eligibility / age requirements
- [ ] Account creation, suspension, termination
- [ ] Payment, billing, subscription, and refund terms (if applicable)
- [ ] Auto-renewal language (FTC Click-to-Cancel compliant)
- [ ] Cancellation method at least as easy as signup
- [ ] Intellectual property ownership (who owns what)
- [ ] User-generated content rights (licence grant)
- [ ] Limitation of liability
- [ ] Disclaimer of warranties
- [ ] Governing law and jurisdiction
- [ ] Arbitration clause (if any) — clickwrap acceptance required
- [ ] Class-action waiver (if any) — clickwrap acceptance required
- [ ] How users will be notified of changes (method and notice period — at least 14 days)
- [ ] UK Consumer Rights Act fairness statement (consumer-facing)
- [ ] Effective date and version number

---

## Cookie Policy (separate from Privacy Policy)

- [ ] What cookies are used and why
- [ ] Cookie categories (strictly necessary, analytics, marketing, personalisation)
- [ ] Which third parties set cookies (named — must match `legal-inventory.json#vendors`)
- [ ] How to manage or opt out (link to consent banner re-open)
- [ ] DUAA 2025 reference if last updated after June 2025 (UK)
- [ ] Last updated date

---

## Refund / Returns Policy (digital or physical goods)

- [ ] What is refundable and under what conditions
- [ ] **UK digital-goods clause:** consumer acknowledges loss of cancellation right before download starts
- [ ] How to request a refund (method, timeframe)
- [ ] Processing time for refunds
- [ ] Exceptions (non-refundable items explicitly listed)
- [ ] Chargeback policy

---

## Acceptable Use Policy (apps with UGC)

- [ ] Prohibited content categories
- [ ] Enforcement mechanism (takedown, suspension, ban)
- [ ] Appeals process
- [ ] DMCA / copyright complaint handoff

---

## DMCA Notice / Designated Agent (US — any app hosting UGC)

- [ ] Designated agent name and contact
- [ ] DMCA notice address (postal + email)
- [ ] Counter-notice procedure
- [ ] Repeat-infringer policy
- [ ] **Registration with US Copyright Office DMCA Designated Agent Directory** (renewable every 3 years)
