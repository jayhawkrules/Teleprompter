# Material-Change Email — Template (Tier 3 trigger)

Use when a Tier 3 change ships outside the annual cycle. Send 14 days before effective date; pair with the in-app forced clickwrap popup that activates on the effective date.

---

## Subject line
Action required: updated Terms for [App Name] — effective [DATE]

## Body

Hi [First Name],

We're making an important change to our [Terms of Service / Privacy Policy] for [App Name], effective **[DATE]**.

**What's changing and why:**
- [Change — plain English, max 30 words, explain user impact]

**What you need to do:**
The next time you sign in after [DATE], we'll ask you to review and accept the updated terms before continuing to use [App Name]. It takes about 30 seconds.

**Read the full updated documents now:**
- Terms of Service: [URL]
- Privacy Policy: [URL]

If you do not agree with the changes, you may close your account at any time before [DATE] by replying to this email or visiting [account-settings URL]. We will delete your data per our Privacy Policy.

Questions? Reply to this email or contact [legal@domain.com].

— The [App Name] Team
Controller of record: Toronado Entertainment, LLC _(or Producing Hollywood, per app)_
