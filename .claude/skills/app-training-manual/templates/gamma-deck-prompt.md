# Gamma Deck Prompts

Ready-to-paste Gamma prompts for portfolio decks. Each prompt follows the format:

> "Create a [N]-slide Gamma presentation for [AUDIENCE] about [APP NAME]. Slides: [list slides]. Tone: [tone]. Include: [key inclusions]."

⚠️ Before using any prompt: open the relevant `references/[app].md` and confirm the feature/pricing/positioning lines are still accurate. Do not paste a deck containing TBC content into a customer- or investor-facing context.

---

## 1. All-Apps Portfolio Overview

**Audience:** investors, prospective hires, general staff onboarding

> Create a 12-slide Gamma presentation for investors and new staff about Andrew Ward's app portfolio under Toronado Entertainment, LLC. Slides: (1) Title — "The Portfolio", (2) The thesis — production-industry tooling, owner-operated, (3) The seven apps at a glance — Mythie, Teleprompter, Aclamos, The Production Shelf, PH Invoicing, ARTAS, Backlot Hub, (4) Mythie / CastHub — casting platform, (5) Teleprompter — creator tool, (6) Aclamos — live event ops, (7) The Production Shelf — curated marketplace, (8) PH Invoicing — production finance, (9) ARTAS — awards submission (10 Lives Studios brand), (10) Backlot Hub — in development, (11) Stack & infrastructure — Firebase, Stripe, house tooling, (12) Closing — what's next. Tone: confident, founder-led, specific. Include: live URLs only for shipped products, "in development" for unshipped, and avoid pricing claims unless explicitly confirmed.

## How to use these prompts

1. Pick the relevant prompt above
2. Open `references/[app].md` and verify Feature Inventory, pricing, and positioning are current
3. Replace any TBC items with confirmed content OR remove that slide
4. Paste into Gamma
5. Review the generated deck against `policy/claims-and-promises.md` before sharing
